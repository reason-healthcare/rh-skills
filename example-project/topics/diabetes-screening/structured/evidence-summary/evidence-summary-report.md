## Key Findings

| # | Finding | Grade |
|---|---------|-------|
| f-001 | Screening should begin at age 35 for all adults and earlier for adults with overweight or obesity plus at least one additional diabetes risk factor. | guideline |
| f-002 | Additional risk factors include first-degree family history, high-risk race or ethnicity, cardiovascular disease, hypertension, low HDL, hypertriglyceridemia, PCOS, physical inactivity, and insulin resistance syndromes such as acanthosis nigricans. | guideline |
| f-003 | Diabetes can be diagnosed by fasting plasma glucose >=126 mg/dL, 2-hour plasma glucose during OGTT >=200 mg/dL, A1C >=6.5 percent, or random plasma glucose >=200 mg/dL with classic hyperglycemia symptoms. | guideline |
| f-004 | Prediabetes is defined by fasting plasma glucose 100-125 mg/dL, 2-hour plasma glucose during OGTT 140-199 mg/dL, or A1C 5.7-6.4 percent. | guideline |
| f-005 | Normal results should trigger repeat screening at a minimum 3-year interval, while prediabetes should trigger referral to an intensive behavioral lifestyle intervention program. | guideline |
