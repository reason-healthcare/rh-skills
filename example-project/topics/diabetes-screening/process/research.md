## Background

Diabetes screening is recommended for adults with risk factors, using fasting
glucose, HbA1c, or oral glucose tolerance tests per ADA criteria.

## Evidence Sources

- **ADA Standards of Care 2024** — primary guideline for screening criteria and
  risk stratification
- **USPSTF Recommendation 2021** — grade B recommendation for screening adults
  35-70 who are overweight/obese
